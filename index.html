About Me

I'm passionate about indigenous culture and art, large scale persistent worlds, and creating immersive sci-fi experiences. I find a lot of beauty in computing and love exploring what emerges from my mistakes. I find simplicity unreasonably valuable, though I rarely manage to achieve it. My goal is to help foster an inclusive and sucessful game development community in Moreton Bay Region.

I live and work on the traditional land of the Ningi Ningi people.
